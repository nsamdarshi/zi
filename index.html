About Zylan

India's Trusted OEM Partner Since 2010

Zylan International is a leading OEM manufacturer of CCTV cameras, IP cameras, PCB modules, and complete surveillance accessories — trusted by 2,500+ companies across India to build and power their own brands.

We specialize in white labelling — meaning businesses, dealers, and distributors can sell our products under their own brand name, logo, and packaging. From PCB modules to finished camera units, everything is available with full OEM customization.
